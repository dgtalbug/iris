Problem

The Work surface exposed only spacious List and Board layouts, navigated away for details, and remained visually empty in repositories without page contracts. It lacked a compact table, shared field hierarchy, context-preserving inspection, and explicit handling for unavailable metadata.

Goal

Provide compact List, Table, and Kanban views over one validated contract projection, with shared filtering and an accessible right-side detail drawer. Preserve deterministic static HTML, file:// operation, no-JavaScript full-page links, light/dark themes, reduced motion, and readable 360 px layouts.
